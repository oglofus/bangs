# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Oglofus Bangs to be welcoming, respectful, and usable by everyone. Contributors and maintainers should participate professionally, assume good intent while addressing impact, and make space for people with different backgrounds, abilities, identities, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and accessibility needs.
- Follow the project's contribution, security, and issue-reporting processes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention without consent.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or bad-faith reporting.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that violates this code privately to [Nikolaos Grammatikos on GitHub](https://github.com/nikosgram). Do not open a public issue containing private or sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed respectfully and as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

If a maintainer is involved in the report, contact another trusted maintainer or the [Oglofus organization](https://github.com/oglofus).

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
